# Quality: the wow / understanding rubric and the two polish phases

Score every scene 0–10 on both axes, as a first-time viewer watching **muted, without knowing the script's language**.
Record a table before and after each phase; never claim a score you haven't looked for.

## Understanding = 10 when all five hold
1. **Mute test:** the picture alone tells what the current line means.
2. **One focus:** exactly one red "look here" element per beat.
3. **Who's who:** every figure's group is clear from its look and behaviour, once its name has been spoken.
4. **Lean text:** ≤ 12 words on screen, headline ≤ 6 words, no sentence said twice (picture and text).
5. **Cause → effect:** when the line says *because/so*, the frame shows the cause, then the result.

## Wow = 10 when all five hold
1. **Always alive:** nothing still > 1.5 s (breathing, blinks, tie sway, pacing).
2. **Signature moment** per scene: a morph or a physical gag drawn from the line.
3. **Built transitions:** the next scene grows out of an object (iris from it, a window that morphs, bubbles that
   collapse into the next scene's knot).
4. **Acting:** anticipation, reaction, landing; expressions change on the word.
5. **Sound:** SFX on the cue for each reveal; music accents on the big reveals (if music is on).

## Phase A: understanding (examples that worked)
Groups revealed on their names · a crossed-out cart for "not just cart" · step-by-step checklist that ticks on each
spoken step with a nod · a window that morphs from the example's site into "our website" ("same as …") · a LEAD
ticket handed over · a features scroll waved off · question bubbles with small pictures, older ones dimmed · the
decided group lands on the spine bar at the end.

## Phase B: wow (examples that worked)
The bar cracks into its shares with a thud · a red X lands like a rubber stamp · the product flies from PURCHASE into
the hand, then a bag · handshake · questions collapse into the next scene · a tangled thread that unravels from the
helper's end as he pulls (two tugs), levels, sinks behind the group and becomes the 60 % bar · blinks · 40–50
cue-locked SFX for ~100 s.

## Scoring procedure (after every video; `scripts/scorecard.py` writes SCORES.md and keeps the history)
`qa_sheet.js` every 2 s (four sheets for ~100 s) + transition/morph midpoints; one row per scene with a reason for
anything under 10. Muted test: write the five questions a viewer should answer and check each is answerable from
frames alone. Report honestly what is not yet 10 and why.
