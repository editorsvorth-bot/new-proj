# Example: The 20-20-60 Split (reference only)

The finished video built with this flow: `scenes.js` (scenes only; helpers come from `assets/kit.js`), `FLOW.md`
(idea units, on-screen words, scene plan on the SRT clock), `PLAN-10.md` (rubric and polish phases) and the SRT.
Rebuild it: `python ../../assets/build_video.py --scenes scenes.js --out the-20-20-60-split.html --title "The 20-20-60 Split"
--file-name the-20-20-60-split --duration 103.728 --mode player --music off --sfx on --voiceover on`.
Never reuse its story, wording or numbers for another script.
