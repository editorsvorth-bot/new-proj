# Plan: understanding 10 and wow 10 on every frame

## 0. What "10" means (the scoring rubric)

Every frame is scored by a first-time viewer watching **muted, without knowing Tamil**.

**Understanding = 10 when all five are true:**
1. **Mute test:** the viewer can say what the current line means from the picture alone.
2. **One focus:** exactly one red "look here" element per beat.
3. **Who's who:** every figure's group is obvious from its look and behaviour, with no label needed.
4. **Lean text:** at most 12 words on screen, a headline of 6 words or fewer, and no sentence repeated by both picture and text.
5. **Cause → effect:** whenever the line says *because* or *so*, the frame shows the cause and then the result.

**Wow = 10 when all five are true:**
1. **Always alive:** nothing is fully still for more than 1.5 s. Even holds have breathing, a head turn or tie sway.
2. **Signature moment:** each scene has one memorable transformation (a morph, a reveal, or a physical gag from the line).
3. **Built transitions:** every scene change grows out of an object (the bar, a window, the tangle), never a plain iris or wipe.
4. **Acting:** characters anticipate, react and land, with expressions changing on the exact word.
5. **Sound:** each reveal has a matching SFX on the cue, and music accents hit the big reveals.

## 4. Phase C: verify (prove it's 10, not just say it)

1. **Automated gates** (added to the build, failing the build if any is broken):
   - every reveal is on a cue;
   - at most 12 words on screen;
   - at most one red focus per beat;
   - no still span over 1.5 s;
   - every scene is fully open on its cue.
2. **Frame grid:** render every 2 s (52 frames) plus each morph and transition midpoint, and score each frame with the rubric in a table (frame, time, cue, U score, W score, failing item).
3. **Mute test:** five questions a viewer should answer after one muted watch:
   - What are the three groups?
   - Why doesn't selling to Never Buy work?
   - How do Definitely Buy people decide?
   - What do Confused Buyers worry about?
   - Why does sales exist?
4. **Fix loop:** fix only the frames scoring under 10, re-render those timestamps, and re-score.
